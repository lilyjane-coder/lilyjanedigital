## TikTok caption

Should you play dead if a black bear attacks? Almost never, and I wish more people knew that.

Playing dead is for one specific situation: a defensive grizzly that made contact because you surprised it or got near cubs. Black bears are a different problem. When a black bear follows you down a trail, circles you, or keeps coming after you back away, that is predatory behaviour, and going limp only makes its job easier. Herrero's research puts predatory encounters behind the majority of fatal black bear attacks.

Here's the part that gets me. The one bear rule everybody can recite is the wrong one for the bear most of us will actually meet. If a black bear that has been stalking you makes contact, fight back with everything you have and aim for the eyes and nose.
